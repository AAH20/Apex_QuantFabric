# Public core, private product and company ownership

Newly authored code in this repository is Apache-2.0. The useful local contracts, oracle, verifier, replay, fixtures and reports remain public. Apache permits commercial reuse and proprietary derivatives; a competitor may build on this code. [License terms](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0.html).

A proposed private product would be separately authored: customer-hosted artifact operations, tenant/identity controls, maintained customer profiles, eligible private integrations, release workflows and support. No private product is claimed here. Customer data, strategies and licensed feeds are not assets to redistribute by default.

The first commercial unit would be a bounded qualification engagement: one authorized workload, baseline/candidate pair, acceptance matrix, evidence package and support period. Actual GPU/DPU and colocation milestones require separately scoped access, operator, instrumentation, rights and cost. No contract price, buyer, savings or revenue is established by the public release.

Equity belongs to a company's separately executed ownership/investment documents. A software license, equipment loan or paid pilot is not automatically an equity transaction. Define background IP, commissioned work, source access, publication rights and payment terms explicitly. Incorporation, chain of title, employee/contractor obligations, licensing and investment require review in the relevant jurisdictions before binding agreements.

Existing Apex projects retain their licenses. ULL is AGPL; no ULL source is included. A process boundary is not an automatic exemption. Intentional dual licensing requires sufficient rights from contributors and dependencies. DCO certifies contribution rights; it is not assignment. [DCO](https://developercertificate.org/), [contributor agreements](https://www.apache.org/licenses/contributor-agreements.html).

Vendor SDK/firmware terms remain separate. Future CUDA/DOCA adapters require version-specific redistribution and benchmark publication review. [CUDA terms](https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/eula/contents.html).
